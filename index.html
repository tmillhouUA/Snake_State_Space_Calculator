What is being counted

Snake. A snake of length k is an ordered sequence of k distinct squares, each orthogonally adjacent to the next, from head to tail. Because head and tail differ, a path and its reverse are two different snakes; for k ≥ 2 the snake count is twice the number of undirected self-avoiding paths with k squares. A length-1 snake is any single square.

Food. One food item on any square the snake does not occupy, so a length-k snake has n² − k food options. Total states = Σk=1…n²−1 snakes(k) · (n² − k).

Left out. Heading direction (only ambiguous for a length-1 snake), score, timing, and reachability. These are configurations, not the states a real game can actually reach from its start position; for example, a real game's snake always arrives at its length by eating, which constrains where food can have been.

Method. Brute-force enumeration of paths blows up around n = 7, so the calculator uses a frontier (transfer-matrix) dynamic program. It sweeps the board square by square, tracking only which edges cross the boundary between processed and unprocessed squares and how their loose ends pair up, with a length-indexed polynomial of exact integer counts per boundary pattern. Results were checked against brute-force enumeration for n ≤ 6.
